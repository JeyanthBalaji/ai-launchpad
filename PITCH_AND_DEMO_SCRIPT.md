## PART A — Written Pitch (for the lablab.ai submission page)

**One-liner:**
AI Launchpad turns one sentence about a business idea into a complete startup
kit — name, landing page, brand, and social captions — in seconds, generated by
a crew of AI agents running on AMD GPUs.

**The problem:**
Starting a business means hours of naming, copywriting, and branding before you
can even show anyone your idea. Founders, students, and creators get stuck at the
blank page.

**The solution:**
Type one line — *"an app that helps students find part-time jobs."* A team of four
specialized AI agents goes to work: a Strategist names it, a Copywriter writes the
landing page, a Brand Designer builds the visual identity, and a Social Manager
drafts launch captions. In about 30 seconds you watch a full brand appear live.

**Why it matters:**
It's the fastest path from idea to launch — a usable starter kit for every founder,
student, and creator.

**Built on AMD:**
Every agent is a focused LLM call served on AMD GPUs via Fireworks AI, and the whole
app is containerized with Docker and deployed on AMD Developer Cloud.

**Tech stack:**
React + Vite + Tailwind (frontend) · Python + FastAPI (backend) · Fireworks AI on
AMD GPUs (the agent crew) · Docker + AMD Developer Cloud (deployment).
